Objective: continue entry context from PR #44 head `dc4dfa2ecf8c7235a0f8d044e639b21648b6d1c3` without changing broker order behavior, exits, sizing, scoring, or risk settings.

The candidate reads the Federal Reserve Board's exact monthly calendar URL for the decision date. It observes listed FOMC Meeting, FOMC Press Conference and FOMC Minutes times. The calendar's release date/time is converted with America/New_York's date-specific offset. No conventional 2pm meeting time is substituted when a published time is missing. An unreadable page, wrong month, malformed event, conflicting identity or unavailable request cannot become an empty successful calendar.

Native schedules now carry `entry_schedule.v2` and policy `exchange_session_listed_bls_fomc.v2`. The existing candidate window (ten minutes before through five minutes after each listed release) applies separately to these events. Post-window entry still needs a completed trigger beginning after the window. Quote refresh rechecks the same rule. Earlier v1 capsules remain explicitly limited to BLS/session evidence. This is a fixed, unproven policy; it does not establish better trades or absence of other risk.
